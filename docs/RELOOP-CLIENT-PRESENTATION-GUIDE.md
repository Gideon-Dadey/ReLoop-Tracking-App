# ReLoop — Client Presentation Script & Guide

> **How to use this script:** Read this document out loud during your meeting or video recording. It is written in the first person ("I") so you can present the app directly to your client screen-by-screen without needing to edit or improvise.

---

## 1. Introduction (The Big Picture)

"Hi! Today I am walking you through **ReLoop**, the mobile application designed for your project. 

ReLoop is a smart circular-economy app that encourages people to **reuse containers instead of using single-use plastic**. Users borrow reusable packaging from local vendors, return them clean, and earn points and rewards for their environmental impact.

Crucially, the app also doubles as a research platform supporting **UN Sustainable Development Goal 12 (SDG 12)** — *Responsible Consumption and Production*. It collects real-time user data through daily pulse questions, baseline surveys, and social media campaign evaluations to help you study what drives sustainable consumer behavior."

---

## 2. Getting Started — Authentication

"First, when users open the app, they arrive at the **Login & Sign Up** screens.

* **Sign Up:** New users create an account with their name, email, and password. This creates their personal eco-profile where all their returns, points, and survey data are saved.
* **Login:** Returning users sign in securely. The app remembers their credentials so their streak and activity history stay intact."

---

## 3. Home Screen — Eco Dashboard

"Once logged in, the user arrives at the **Home Dashboard**. This is the core command center of the app.

* **Header:** At the top, we show today's date, a personalized greeting, notifications, and profile access.
* **Impact Score Card:** This single score combines the user's total points, plastic saved, and carbon offset into one gamified rating. Next to it, they can see their active daily streak and total earned points.
* **Borrow & Return Cards:** Here are the primary actions:
  * **Borrow** shows how many containers the user currently has on loan.
  * **Return** shows their total lifetime returns and opens the scanner.
* **Weekly Goal & Milestones:** We feature a progress bar (e.g., 5 returns per week) and achievement badges like *Eco Starter* or *Green Champion* to keep users motivated.
* **Real-World Impact:** We translate their stats into understandable metrics — like liters of water saved, kilometers of driving emissions offset, and plastic bottles kept out of landfills.
* **AI Coach:** Below, our automated AI coach offers smart tips on properly cleaning and returning containers."

---

## 4. Activity Tab — Tracking Borrowed Items

"Next, let's look at the **Activity** tab. This acts as a personal logbook split into two views:

* **Active Holds:** Shows every container the user currently has out on loan, including the vendor location, return deadline, and a direct **Return Now** button.
* **History:** A complete timestamped ledger of past borrows and returns, showing points earned and unique container serial IDs for full transparency."

---

## 5. Scan QR — Collection bases + container return

"The center button is **Scan QR**. There are two codes:

1. **Collection-base QR** (on the bin or store) — opens that collection page, sets it as the drop-off, and lists nearest partners in the **same group**.
2. **Container QR** — if no base is set yet, the app shows nearest collection bases first. If a base is already set, it goes straight to the cleanliness photo.

Then: photo check → return is logged **at that collection base** → points post.

Print these payloads as QRs for demo:

* `reloop://collection/green-market-hq` — Accra Central
* `reloop://collection/eco-hub-store` — Accra Central (same group)
* `reloop://collection/sustainability-corner` — Airport City
* `RE-001` — a container serial"

---

## 6. Research Hub — SDG 12 & Academic Data Collection

"Now, let me show you the **Research** tab — which powers the academic side of your project.

* **Research Hub Overview:** At the top, the user sees their research streak, campaign evaluation count, and overall SDG 12 alignment score.
* **Daily Pulse:** A 30-second daily survey that rotates through 21 key behavioral questions to track shifts in eco-attitudes over time.
* **Community Survey:** A one-time 10-question research survey establishing baseline consumer behavior.
* **Campaign Evaluations:** Users can submit links to sustainability posts from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or Pinterest, and rate them across key criteria like design, message clarity, circular economy focus, and call-to-action effectiveness."

---

## 7. Analytics & Campaign Dashboard

"From the Research section, we open the **Campaign Dashboard**. 

This visual analytics tool aggregates all user evaluations into live charts showing:
* Average scores across design, message, and call-to-action.
* Social media platform breakdowns (Instagram vs. TikTok vs. Facebook).
* Top-performing campaigns.
* An overall **SDG 12 Alignment Percentage**.

This gives you ready-to-present visual data for your project defense without needing manual spreadsheet calculations."

---

## 8. Map Tab — Partner Locations

"Moving to the **Map** tab, this connects the app to physical locations.

* **Interactive Pins:** Users can see nearby partner cafes and drop-off stations.
* **Material Filters:** They can filter locations by material type — Plastic, Glass, Metal, or E-Waste.
* **Directions:** Tapping a pin reveals vendor details, opening hours, accepted items, and navigation routes."

---

## 9. Community & Eco Network

"Under **Eco Network**, users can see the community leaderboard. 

* It ranks top eco-contributors by points and plastic saved.
* It displays total community-wide impact, showing users that they are part of a larger collective effort."

---

## 10. Settings & Customization

"Finally, in **Settings**, users can:
* Edit their profile picture and personal details.
* Toggle between **Light and Dark themes**.
* Manage notification preferences, security settings, and password updates.
* Access the **How to Return** tutorial guide."

---

## 11. Quick Summary (For Slides & Wrap-Up)

> "In summary, **ReLoop** combines real-world container tracking, gamified rewards, and AI cleanliness verification with academic research tools aligned with **SDG 12**. It provides an engaging experience for users while generating valuable behavioral data for your project."

---

## 12. Quick Answers to Common Presentation Questions

* **Q: How does ReLoop prevent people from returning dirty containers?**
  * *Answer:* Users must take a photo of the container, which undergoes AI verification before points are granted.
* **Q: How does the app support SDG 12 research?**
  * *Answer:* Through daily pulse surveys, baseline questionnaires, and user evaluations of social media sustainability campaigns that generate live dashboard analytics.
* **Q: How are eco metrics calculated?**
  * *Answer:* Each verified container return translates into standard formulas for plastic weight avoided (kg), CO₂ emissions offset, and equivalent water/driving savings.
