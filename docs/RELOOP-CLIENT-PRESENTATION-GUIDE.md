# ReLoop — Client Presentation Guide

**Who this is for:** You (the developer) explaining the app to your client, and your client (the student) defending her project to lecturers or examiners.

**How to use this document:**
- Read it once before your meeting on Monday.
- Walk through the app on a phone while reading each section — section order matches what you see on screen.
- The **“Say this to your client”** lines are simple sentences she can repeat in her own words during a defense.
- You do **not** need to understand coding to explain ReLoop. Focus on **what the app does** and **why it matters**.

---

## 1. What is ReLoop? (The big picture)

**ReLoop** is a mobile app that helps people **reuse containers instead of throwing them away**. Think of it like a library for food/drink containers: you **borrow** a container, use it, **return** it clean, and earn **points** for doing the right thing for the environment.

The app also supports **research** linked to **UN Sustainable Development Goal 12 (SDG 12)** — *Responsible Consumption and Production*. It collects information about how social media campaigns influence sustainable behaviour, through surveys and campaign evaluations.

**Say this to your client:**

> “ReLoop is an eco-friendly app that rewards people for returning reusable containers. It tracks their environmental impact, shows nearby drop-off locations on a map, and includes a research section where users can evaluate sustainability campaigns and answer surveys for my academic project.”

|--------|----------------|

## 6. Scan QR (centre tab)

**Purpose:** The main action screen — how users physically interact with containers in the real world.

### How the return flow works (3 steps)

| Step | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| **1. SCAN** | Point the camera at the container’s QR code. |
| **2. PHOTO** | Take a photo of the clean container (proves it’s ready to return). |
| **3. VERIFY** | The app checks the photo with AI; if clean, the return is recorded and points are added. |

On success:
- Points are awarded (typically around 10 points per clean return).
- Home and Activity screens update.
- A notification confirms success.

If verification fails, the user is asked to clean the container and try again.

**Say this to your client:**

> “Scan QR is the heart of the app. Users scan the container, take a photo to prove it’s clean, and the app verifies it before counting the return. That stops people from returning dirty containers and keeps the circular system trustworthy.”

## 9. Evaluate Campaigns (detail)

From **Research → Evaluate Campaigns**:

### Campaign list
- Shows submitted campaigns with platform icon (Instagram, TikTok, etc.).
- **Pending** = not yet scored.
- **Score ring** = already evaluated (score out of 5).

### Submit a new campaign
User provides:
- Title
- Platform (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest)
- URL link to the post
- Optional description

### Evaluate a campaign (scoring)
When opening a campaign, users rate it on criteria such as:
- Visual design quality
- Narrative / story coherence
- Alignment with circular economy ideas
- Behavioural call to action (does it tell people what to *do*?)
- Community orientation

These scores feed the **Campaign Dashboard** charts.

**Say this to your client:**

> “Users don’t just scroll campaigns — they critically evaluate them using a structured rubric. That produces measurable data on which sustainability messages work best on which platforms.”

## 19. Likely examiner questions — and simple answers

**Q: What problem does ReLoop solve?**  
A: Single-use plastic waste. The app makes reuse easy, rewarding, and measurable.

**Q: How is this different from a normal recycling app?**  
A: Recycling happens after waste is created. ReLoop focuses on **reuse before waste** — borrow, return, repeat — plus research on what messages change behaviour.

**Q: Where does the research data come from?**  
A: Campaign evaluations, Daily Pulse answers, and the one-time Research Survey — all inside the app.

**Q: How do you know containers are actually returned clean?**  
A: Users scan a QR code and submit a photo; AI verification checks cleanliness before points are awarded.

**Q: What is SDG 12 doing in the app?**  
A: Campaigns are scored for alignment with responsible consumption. The dashboard shows an SDG 12 alignment percentage across evaluated campaigns.

**Q: Is user data safe?**  
A: Accounts use secure login; survey data is aggregated for research; users control profile and notification settings in Settings.

**Q: Can this work in the real world?**  
A: Yes — the Map connects to partner vendors, Scan QR handles real returns, and Activity logs every transaction.

## 20. One-paragraph project summary (for slides or handouts)

> **ReLoop** is a mobile sustainability platform that promotes reusable container systems through gamified returns, real-time impact tracking, and location-based partner maps. Users borrow and return containers via QR scanning and AI-assisted cleanliness verification, earning points, streaks, and milestones that translate environmental impact into understandable metrics. Integrated research tools — including campaign evaluation, daily pulse surveys, and a structured research questionnaire — support SDG 12-aligned study of how social media influences responsible consumption behaviour. Analytics dashboards and shareable impact cards turn individual and community data into insights suitable for academic presentation and public engagement.
