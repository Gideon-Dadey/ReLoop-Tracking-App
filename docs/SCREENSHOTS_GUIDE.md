### Dark Mode vs Light Mode

Take at least these screens in **both** modes:
- Home Dashboard
- Research Tab
- Campaign Dashboard
- Survey Question

### How to Take Screenshots

**On Android (Expo Go):**
- Press **Power + Volume Down** simultaneously
- Or use the notification shade screenshot button

**On iOS:**
- Press **Side Button + Volume Up** simultaneously

### Tips for Professional Screenshots

1. Make sure the phone has good signal/Wi-Fi icon showing
2. Set the time to something clean (e.g., 9:41 AM — Apple's classic)
3. Clear notifications before taking screenshots
4. Have realistic data in the app (not "test" or "asdf" entries)
5. Use meaningful campaign titles like:
   - "Zero Waste Kitchen Challenge"
   - "Plastic Free July TikTok Series"
   - "Reusable Cup Movement"
   - "Campus Sustainability Drive"
